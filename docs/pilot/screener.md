# Recruiting screener (DRAFT)

Use as a short Google Form, or ask the questions in a DM. Takes about 3 minutes. **Draft: not published.**

Who we want: a mix of curricula (Ambleside, Memoria Press, Orthodox/other faith-based, eclectic), a mix of family sizes, **strangers preferred** (not friends or family of DeeDee), and **a good share of people who plan mostly on their phone**.

---

**Intro text for the form:**
> Thanks for your interest! We're building a planning tool for homeschool families and want to watch a few parents try an early version. Sessions are 30 minutes over video. These questions help us pick a mix of families. We only use your answers to schedule the session.

### About your homeschool
1. How many children are you homeschooling this year? *(1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5+)*
2. What ages or grades? *(free text)*
3. Which curriculum or approach do you mainly use? *(Ambleside Online / Memoria Press / Orthodox or other faith-based / Classical (other) / Charlotte Mason (other) / Eclectic or mix / Other: ___)*
4. How many years have you been homeschooling? *(First year / 1–2 / 3–5 / 6+)*

### How you plan today
5. How do you plan your school year now? *(choose all)* Paper planner / Printed curriculum schedule / Spreadsheet / A planning app (which?) / I mostly wing it / Other
6. When you plan or check off work, what do you usually use? *(Mostly phone / Mostly computer / Mostly tablet / Mostly paper / A mix)*
7. What phone do you have? *(iPhone / Android / Other / No smartphone)*
8. In the last month, how often did you fall behind on the plan and have to rearrange? *(Never / Once or twice / Most weeks / Constantly)*
9. In one or two sentences, what's the most annoying part of keeping your school year on track? *(free text: the most useful answer on the form)*

### The session
10. Can you join a 30-minute video call (Zoom or Google Meet) and share your screen, or join from your phone? *(Yes, computer / Yes, phone / Not sure)*
11. Which times usually work for you? *(Weekday mornings / Weekday afternoons / Weekday evenings / Weekends)* + time zone
12. How do you know DeeDee? *(I don't, I saw a group post / Acquaintance / Friend or family)*. We prefer people who don't know her well, so the feedback is honest.
13. First name and best way to reach you (email or how you'd like a reply in the group). *(Used only to schedule this session.)*

---

**Selection notes (for DeeDee, not shown on the form):**
- Aim for 5–6 sessions. Rough mix: at least 2 per group (Ambleside, Memoria Press, Orthodox), at least 3 phone-first planners (Q6), at least 1 with 4+ kids, at least 1 first-year family.
- Prefer "I don't know her" on Q12. Use friends only to fill gaps, and note it in the results.
- Q8 = "Most weeks"/"Constantly" are the core audience. Include at least one "Never" for contrast.
- Long, specific answers on Q9 usually mean a good session.
