# Facilitator checklist: 30-minute remote session (DRAFT)

## A few days before
- [ ] Confirm the time, time zone, and video link with the participant (DeeDee sends this herself).
- [ ] Ask if they'll join from phone or computer. Phone users: ask them to join from the phone they plan on, if they can.
- [ ] Decide what they'll test (prototype link or the current build), and seed the demo family data.
- [ ] Check that the link opens on iPhone Safari, Android Chrome, and a desktop browser.
- [ ] Prepare 3–4 tasks (below) and a notes template.
- [ ] Ask permission to record (written OK or a yes on the call before recording). Note it if they say no.

## 10 minutes before
- [ ] Reset the demo data to its starting state.
- [ ] Close notifications, other tabs, anything private on your screen.
- [ ] Have the notes template open. Optional: a second person takes notes so you can watch.
- [ ] Water, quiet room, timer visible.

## Intro (3 min)
- [ ] Thank them. Say it's 30 minutes and you'll stop on time.
- [ ] "We're testing the app, not you. If something is confusing, that's our fault and it's exactly what we need to hear."
- [ ] "Please think out loud: what you expect, what you're looking for, what surprises you."
- [ ] "I'll try not to help much. If I answer a question with a question, that's why."
- [ ] Get recording consent on the record (or confirm no recording).

## Warm-up (4 min)
- [ ] "Tell me about your school year. How many kids, what curriculum?"
- [ ] "How do you plan the year now? Can you show me (on paper or your phone)?"
- [ ] "Tell me about the last time you got behind. What did you do?"

## Tasks (18 min). Don't explain the screen first.
- [ ] **First look (2 min):** "What do you think this screen is showing you?" Let them talk. Don't correct them.
- [ ] **Find something:** "When will [child] finish [book]?"
- [ ] **Life happens:** "[Child] was sick all next week. Show me what you'd do." Watch whether they find the week override, and whether "keep end date vs push end date" makes sense in their words.
- [ ] **Log today:** "You just finished today's reading. How would you mark that?" (Especially on phones.)
- [ ] **Their own year (if time):** "Would this fit how your year really works? What's missing?"
- [ ] After each task: "How sure are you that worked?" (1–5) and "What did you expect to happen?"

## Wrap-up (5 min)
- [ ] "What was the most confusing part?" / "What was the most useful part?"
- [ ] "What would you call [the timeline / an override / a lane]?" Write down their words.
- [ ] "Would you use this instead of what you do now? Why or why not?"
- [ ] "Anything I should have asked?"
- [ ] Thank them. Mention any thank-you gift if one was promised. Stop on time.

## Facilitator rules of thumb
- Don't lead ("Did you see the button up top?"). Ask "What are you looking for?"
- Silence is fine. Count to 5 before stepping in.
- If they're stuck for more than 2 minutes, give one small hint, note it, and move on.
- Write down exact quotes and where they tapped, not your interpretation.

## Right after (15 min, while it's fresh)
- [ ] Fill in the notes template: device, curriculum, kids, task results (success / with a hint / failed), top 3 quotes, top 3 problems.
- [ ] Note any bugs separately from usability problems.
- [ ] Store the recording and notes in one private place. Delete the recording when the pilot is done, if that's what you promised.

## After all sessions
- [ ] Tally the task results across participants.
- [ ] List problems seen in 2+ sessions. These decide the Sprint 2–3 tickets (now "pending pilot results").
- [ ] Collect the words parents used. Use them for labels.
