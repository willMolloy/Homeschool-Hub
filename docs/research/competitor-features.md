# Competitor feature survey

Researched 2026-11-04 (web research by an agent; nobody was contacted). Prices are as shown on each maker's page on that date. Anything not read from a primary page is marked *(unverified)*.

## Homeschool Hub today (for comparison)

Year timeline of subject bars per child, Week view, auto-pacing engine (reflows around days off and events, assignment weekdays), effort shown as bar height, per-subject colours, Records (log setup, entries in hours and minutes, books, totals for a range, CSV export with chosen columns), days off and recurring events. Planned (ROADMAP): weekly briefing with finish forecasts, undo, week adjust, bulk add, copy and CSV import, calendar templates, year rollover, separate parent logins, a read-only share link, dark mode. Later modules: daily checklist, ICS export, quick-capture portfolio, curriculum budget, co-op groups. Not planned: native app or full offline, curriculum templates, print, payments. Target price under discussion: about $39–49 a year.

## Gaps for a "one-stop shop"

Homeschool Hub's moat is the year map plus steering. Every paid competitor, though, ships grades, report cards, transcripts and printable reports. Those are the table stakes it lacks.

### Prioritized short list

| # | Gap | Why | Effort | Roadmap fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Printable and PDF year-end records (attendance, hours, books, summary) | Every competitor prints; evaluators and umbrella schools want paper, and CSV alone feels unfinished | S–M | Fits Records (a printable summary is already mentioned); before the friends beta |
| 2 | Simple grades and report cards (optional per subject, letter or percent, narrative for CM) | Table stakes in 8 of 9 paid tools; CM families want narrative over grades | M | New optional module; after spring term, before going public |
| 3 | High-school transcript with GPA and course descriptions | A strong reason families pay (Homeschool Fox charges $29 for it); decision P2 already asks this | M | Before going public; could be the paid upsell |
| 4 | Read-only evaluator or grandparent share | Already planned; matches Fox's evaluator letters and Panda's family portal | S–M | Already planned before the beta, keep it there |
| 5 | Daily checklist with student check-off (kid view, no login) | Universal in competitors; lets kids own their day without parent input | M | Already an optional module; worth promoting to the beta |
| 6 | ICS calendar export, later 2-way | Planet's two-way sync is a favourite; families live in Google or Apple calendars | S export, L 2-way | Already planned as later; pull the one-way export earlier |
| 7 | State compliance helpers (required days, hours and subjects per state, with alerts) | Fox's whole pitch; P6 already asks which states first | M per state set | Before going public; start with DeeDee's and the friends' states |
| 8 | Photo portfolio quick capture tied to subjects and days | Panda and Tracker portfolios; CM narrations and nature-journal pages | M–L (storage, hosting cost) | Later module; watch the $20/mo budget |

Honourable mentions: plan templates and sharing among friends (CM book lists; partly covered by calendar templates and copy), budget and ESA receipts (Panda; planned later), and chores or a household calendar (Planet; it dilutes the "steering tool" focus, so skip for now). A native app is not needed: Tracker and Manager sell without one, and Planet's app is its most-complained-about piece.
