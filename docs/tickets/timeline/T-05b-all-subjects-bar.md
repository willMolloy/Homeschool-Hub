# T-05b — "All subjects" bar pinned above the lanes

**Source:** DeeDee's feedback on the T-04 screenshots (2026-10-28): "make it obvious how heavy the whole day or week is across all lines combined". **Depends on:** T-05. **Size:** S.

## Goal

A parent can see at a glance how heavy each day or week is across every subject and child. One grey bar at the top of the timeline shows it.

## Context

T-04 drew a "Total" strip under the last lane. It used the model's summed series (`model.totals`, daily and weekly-smoothed) and was normalised so the busiest point fills 32 px. At the bottom it was easy to miss, and it was drawn at 0.35 opacity rather than with the bar token.

## Steps

1. `layout.ts`:
   - The totals block now comes right after the axis and event strips: a label row, then the 32 px bar on its own fixed centerline, then a subject gap, then the lanes (`lanesTop`).
   - Family-wide breaks span the bar and the lanes.
   - `totalsPxPerPoint(totals, maxPx)` is the normalisation: the heaviest point across both the daily and weekly series equals `TOTALS_HEIGHT_PX` (32 px, within the 28–36 px asked for). Every zoom therefore shares one scale.
2. `Timeline.tsx`:
   - The label "All subjects" is first in the label group, so it is read first.
   - The bar is drawn before the lane bars.
   - Grid lines and Today run down to the bottom of the lanes.
3. Tokens: a new neutral chrome colour `--tl-total` (light `oklch(0.74 0.008 250)`, dark `oklch(0.56 0.01 260)`) at `--tl-bar-opacity`, generated by `scripts/palette.ts`.

## Done when

- [ ] The "All subjects" bar sits above every lane at every zoom, and never overlaps a lane label or row.
- [ ] Its thickness at each point is the sum of all lanes, scaled so the family's heaviest point is 32 px. It uses the weekly series at Year and the daily series when zoomed in, the same as the lanes.
- [ ] It is neutral grey at the shared bar opacity.
- [ ] **Only what's essential:** one label ("All subjects") and one bar. No legend, scale, numbers or extra text.
- [ ] Screenshots at 390 and 1280 px.

## Tests

  - it renders at every zoom with a single "All subjects" label
- `apps/web/test/timeline-view.test.tsx`: the labels now start with "All subjects".

## Decisions

- **"Pinned"** means placed at the top of the picture, not sticky while the page scrolls. Sticky SVG rows need a second SVG or an HTML overlay. This is conservative, and easy to revisit if the long phone page hides it.
- **One scale for every zoom**, taken from the heaviest point across both series, so the bar's thickness means the same thing at Year and Fall.
- **"All subjects"** rather than "Total": it fits comfortably on a 390 px phone and says what is summed.
- **Smoothing:** this ticket keeps the same weekly and daily series as the lanes. The smooth-curve outline comes in T-05c, for the lanes and this bar together.

## Out of scope

- smooth outlines and the Month zoom (T-05c)
- per-child totals
- numbers or a scale
