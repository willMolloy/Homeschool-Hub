# S10-11 — Twelve soft subject colours, each subject its own
**Source:** DeeDee's decision 2 (2026-11-03); year-polish-notes decision 9 (Art to coral); UX review improvement 9 · **Depends on:** none · **Size:** S

## Goal
The subject palette grows from 8 to 12 soft hues that meet AA in light and dark, and each new subject is given a colour no other active subject uses.

## Context
- **Decided by DeeDee, 2026-11-03:**
  - every subject gets its own auto-assigned colour;
  - the palette grows from 8 to about 12 soft hues that meet AA in light and dark;
  - colours repeat only once a family has more subjects than the palette holds, and the icon badges (S8-11) tell those apart.
- Today, in `features/roster/palette.ts`:
  - `PALETTE` holds 8 stored swatches;
  - `SWATCH_SLOT` maps them to Soft Paper slots 1–8 (`--tl-subject-N-*`);
  - `slotOfColor` sends any other hex to the nearest swatch;
  - `nextUnusedColor` already picks the first unused swatch.
- `PALETTE_SLOTS = 8` in `features/timeline/model.ts`.
- The tokens are generated by `apps/web/scripts/palette.ts` and checked in `palette.test.ts`.
- In the demo family, Art (`#5c8a2f`) maps to the same green as Math. Decision 9 moves Art to coral (`#b0482f`, slot 6).

## Steps
1. Add 4 soft hues as slots 9–12, generated in light and dark with their label and strong tones. They should stay apart from the existing 8 (for example rose, apricot, aqua and plum, checked with the colour-blind preview from T-01b).
2. Add 4 stored swatches to `PALETTE` and `PALETTE_NAMES`, map them in `SWATCH_SLOT`, and set `PALETTE_SLOTS = 12`.
3. Have `nextUnusedColor` choose among the 12, so a new subject (in Subjects, the Add assignment "New subject…" path and the seed) gets a colour no active subject has.
4. Set Art's colour to `#b0482f` in the demo seed (`db/demo-family.ts`).
5. Leave existing families' stored colours as they are. A parent changes them in S10-12.

## Done when
- The palette test passes for all 12 slots in light and dark (AA text on each fill).
- A family adding a 9th to 12th subject gets a new colour each time.
- In the demo family, every subject has its own colour.
- Only what's essential: no change to colours parents already chose.

## Tests
- Web: the palette test covers 12 slots; `nextUnusedColor` finds every unused colour and only cycles once all 12 are used; the stored colour of each new swatch maps to its own slot.
- Seed: Art is coral in the demo family.

## Out of scope
- The Subjects screen controls (S10-12).
- Making children's colours unique.
