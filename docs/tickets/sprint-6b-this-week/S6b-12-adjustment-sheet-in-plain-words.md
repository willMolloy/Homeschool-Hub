# S6b-12 — The week adjustment sheet in plain words

**Finding:** Sprint 6 L1, L2 (`reviews/sprint-6-verification.md`)  
**Depends on:** none  
**Size:** XS

## Goal
The week adjustment sheet labels the week correctly and shows each amount once, in whole, readable numbers.

## Steps
1. Label rows by the shown week: "This week" for the current week, "That week" (or the dates, "Nov 2–8") for a coming one.
2. Round starting amounts to the unit's step (whole pages, lessons and chapters; 5 minutes), and show the amount once per row (the stepper's value), not twice.

## Done when
- A coming week's rows don't say "This week"; no row shows "4.38 lessons" or repeats its amount.

## Tests
- Web: the label for current and coming weeks; rounded starting values; one amount per row.

## Out of scope
- The redesigned sheet (held with S6b-02/03).
