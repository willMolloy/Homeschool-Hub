# Sprint 16 — The week, day by day, and clearer Records

> **Status: approved concept, after Sprint 15** (Will, 2026-11-05: "I like the concept. Want to see it in practice to see if it is too full of a screen"). Build it, then show Will real screenshots (phone and desktop, a busy two-child week) before tuning. One branch for the sprint; PR after its tester passes.

## Decisions
- Will wants every day's specific work for the week **in one view, without navigating between days**.
- Schedule · Week gets a **Bars | Days** toggle. **Bars stays the default** until Will has seen Days in practice; the choice is remembered per device.
- **Days:** on a phone, the days stack in one scroll; on wide screens (840 px and up), the days sit side by side as columns. Each assignment is one line: subject icon, title, and that day's range in whole units (S15-06). Each day header has a small load bar (no numbers, no minutes). Days off and breaks are one quiet line. Under All children, each line carries the child's initial and colour dot.
