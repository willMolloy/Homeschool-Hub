# S16-02 — The Bars | Days toggle and All children markers
**Size:** S
## Steps
1. A Segmented toggle **Bars | Days** in the Week header row (44 px, fits beside the week arrows at 360 px or wraps cleanly under them). Bars is the default; the choice is remembered on the device.
2. Under All children, each Days line shows the child's initial in their colour dot; with one child selected, no marker.
3. Viewers (Sprint 15) get the toggle too (read-only).
## Done when
- The toggle switches views and is remembered after reload; markers show only under All children.
## Tests
- Web: toggle, persistence, markers.
