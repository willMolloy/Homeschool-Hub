# S16-08 — Chapters that take more than one day say start and finish

**Source:** Will, 2026-11-05: The Hobbit showed "Keep going: Chapter 17" on Monday and "Chapter 17" on Wednesday; "count how many days will be spent on the chapter … first half … finish". Also closes Sprint 15 N3.  
**Size:** S
## Steps
1. In the shared whole-unit day-range helper (S15-06), when one chapter (or lesson or page) spans several of an assignment's scheduled days, label those days by position: first day "Chapter 17 · start", middle days "Chapter 17 · day 2 of 3", last day "Chapter 17 · finish". Two days: "start" and "finish".
2. Days covering whole units keep "Chapter 18" / "Chapters 18–19". "Keep going" is no longer used.
3. Days count only the assignment's own scheduled days (its weekdays, minus days off and breaks).
4. Applies everywhere ranges show: Days view, Week bars, weekly and daily sheets. Display only; the plan is unchanged.
## Done when
- A book at about 0.5 chapters a day on Mon/Wed shows "Chapter 17 · start" then "Chapter 17 · finish"; at about 0.33 a day, "start", "day 2 of 3", "finish"; Days, bars and sheets agree.

- Web: the helper (2-day, 3-day, mixed with whole-chapter days, days off inside a span, last chapter of the book); Days and sheet equality.
