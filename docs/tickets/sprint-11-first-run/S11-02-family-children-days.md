# S11-02 — Family, children and school days
**Size:** S
## Steps
1. **Step 1, your family:** family name; "Our week starts on" Monday (default) or Sunday.
2. **Step 2, your children:** a name field and "Add another" (each child gets the next colour). At least one child is required to continue; this step has no Skip. Children added here appear in Set up → Children.
3. **Step 3, school days:** the seven day chips, Mon–Fri pre-selected; at least one day.
## Done when
- A parent sets the name, adds two children and picks Mon–Thu; Set up shows the same.
## Tests
- Web: required child; chips; saves through the existing family and children APIs.
