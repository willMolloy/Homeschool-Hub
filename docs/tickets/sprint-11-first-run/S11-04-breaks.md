# S11-04 — Breaks: add, later or skip
**Size:** S
## Steps
1. Step 5 lists US holidays in the year's range to tick (none ticked by default) and "Add a break" (name and dates).
2. Three buttons: **Add breaks** (saves ticked holidays and added breaks), **I'll get back to you on this** (saves nothing, leaves a Home checklist reminder "Add your breaks"), **Skip** (saves nothing, no reminder).
3. If the year was left for later, holidays use the current school year's months (Aug–Jun).
## Done when
- Each button behaves as above; added breaks show under Days off.
## Tests
- Web: the three paths; reminder only for "get back to you".
