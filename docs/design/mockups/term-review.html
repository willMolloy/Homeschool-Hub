Term in review (Sprint 13, S13-05)

One keepsake card per child, for a term or the year. It shows only what the family really tracks and the parent really marked: assignments marked finished, books read, time by subject and school days. Nothing is estimated, scored or graded. The card is drawn at its saved size (1080 × 1350, portrait 4:5) and scaled down here. Notes: ../term-review-notes.md.
