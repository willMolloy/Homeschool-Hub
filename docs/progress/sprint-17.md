## Decisions

- **S17-01 N3 wording:** the ticket says "Finish Chapter 8"; the Sprint 16 words are "Chapter 8 · finish" (S16-08, S16-11), and that is what the last day of a 0.4-a-day book reads. Kept that one convention.
- **S17-01 week filter** applies to the Week view's briefing only. Home's one-line briefing is for the whole family and is unchanged.
- **S17-01 N2 rule:** a row needs the line above only when none of its assignments gets any name placed (inside, cut, or to the right); an assignment already named to the right of its last block, or inside its wide post-break block, keeps that. Only daily (This month) detail; Term and Year are untouched. Two thin-row names on one line never overlap (the later one is dropped).
- **S17-01 day-off words:** the Kind list's options changed from "Vacation / Other" to "Break / Day off" so a kind and its label-less title are the same word everywhere.
- **S17-01 Rose only:** Lilac keeps its recorded values (a test pins the first eight light fills); Rose moved toward red-salmon, and stays at least 7 from every other fill (the palette's own rule).
- **S17-01 noindex** is on every join page (new family, parent, viewer), not only the view-link one, because the page is the secret link in all three cases.
- **S17-01 N5:** the framework's parameter limit is raised rather than special-casing the join routes, so any route with a long id param answers with its own 404 or 400, not a 414.
- **S17-02 404 for reads, 400 for writes:** the ticket says 404 for the routes that took an id and answered 200 with nothing; a write's `childIds` or `subjectId` in a body is a different contract (400 `invalid_reference`, S2a-05) that the forms and tests rely on, so it stays. `GET /api/weights/suggestion` and `GET /api/log-entries/run-days` already refused another account's ids (400) and are unchanged.
- **S17-02 one clearer message than before:** another account's id and a made-up id are the same 404, so the answer says nothing about which exists.
- **S17-03 through the API, not SQL:** the demo seed writes SQL; this family is made with the real routes (and real invites for the second parent, the viewers and the co-op), so it cannot hold a state the app would refuse. The cost is a dependency on the API code, which is the point.
- **S17-03 fixed date:** everything is dated as of Nov 5, 2026 (the real date today); the run is easiest that week. Later, Home simply asks about more finished assignments.
- **S17-03 the Fall and Spring views** are the built-in ones renamed to the exact terms through the zoom-view overrides, so Records' ranges and Term in review read Aug 17 to Dec 18 and Jan 4 to May 21.
- **S17-03 Rachel and Tom open the Hartley family** at sign-in: the app opens the account used last, and joining the co-op as viewers used it, so the script marks the family as used last.
- **S17-03 logins:** Rachel, Tom (also a viewer of the co-op), Grandma Joan (viewer) and the co-op lead.
- **S17-04 a new family for Part 1:** the seeded family has already set up, so the first-run flow is walked with a second family made by `create-family-invite`; Part 2 then adds books to that family.
- **S17-04 not walked end to end:** the steps are written from the screens as built and from the seeded data (Home, Week, Records numbers were read in a browser); the tester pass walks the first third.
