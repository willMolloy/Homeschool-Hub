## Decisions

- **S9-01 Records is a placeholder** ("Records — coming soon") until the later Records tickets.
- **S9-01 Track work code stays, unlinked.** `routes/TrackPage.tsx`, `features/track/` and the check-in hooks are untouched but nothing routes to them: `/track` (with its query) redirects to `/schedule?view=week`. A finished week can still be checked in from Schedule · Week by stepping back a week (that board is the same component). Its tests render the page directly (`renderTrack` in `test/render.tsx`).
- **S9-01 the unanswered-check-in nudge** on Schedule · Week ("Last week isn't checked in yet.") stays for now and links to `/schedule?view=week` (it used to link to `/track`). Home never showed it (Briefing `showNudge={false}`) and still does not.
- **S9-01 Home** has two equal cards, Schedule (unchanged content) and Records ("Attendance, hours and books"; no totals until Records exists). The Track work card, and with it its "All done" beside "needs a quick check" contradiction, is gone.
- **S9-01 changed test expectations.** `css-contrast.test.ts`: the `.home-track i` and `.home-dot` selectors are dropped from its non-text list because the per-child progress bar and the check-in dot they styled are gone (the test insists every listed selector exists). `app-shell.test.tsx`: the tabs are Home, Schedule, Records (was Home, Track work, Schedule), the active-tab and one-tap tests use Records. `router.test.tsx`: `/records` shows "Records" (was `/track` showing "Track work"); new cases that `/track` and `/track?week=...` land on `/schedule` with `view=week` and the query kept. `home.test.tsx`: the two Track work card tests (per-child "N left", "Last week needs a quick check") are replaced by one test that Home has exactly the Schedule and Records cards; the new-family test looks for Records; the tab-names and 44 px card-link tests say Schedule and Records; the briefing test waits for the Records link instead of the child's name (no per-child rows now). `s8b-fixes.test.tsx` N9 ("Nothing planned, not All done" on Home) now checks Home shows neither line, since the per-child progress rows no longer exist; the N10 test renders the Track page directly. `briefing.test.tsx`: the nudge link goes to `/schedule?view=week`, and the test that followed it to Track work and confirmed there is removed (that page has no route; S9-02 retires the card and page for good). `undo-bar.test.tsx` leaves the page by the Records tab. `track.test.tsx`: renders the page directly (`renderTrack`) instead of at `/track`; assertions unchanged.

- **S9-04 DateField is a text box plus a calendar button**, not a sheet: a nested sheet inside the Add assignment sheet and the fixed Year edit bar would fight over focus, so the calendar hangs under the field (above it for the edit bar), as wide as a phone allows, and swaps to the field's right edge when the left would pass the screen or sheet (the End field). Dates read "Nov 1, 2026"; "Nov 1" where tight; the week header "Oct 26 – Nov 1"; Home's date "Sunday, Nov 1". Weeks in the calendar start on Sunday (US).
- **S9-04 typing:** "11/1/2026", "Nov 1, 2026" and "2026-11-01" are read, but only with a four-digit year and a real day, so half a date never sets a different one. Text kept as typed while focused; tidied on leaving. Emptying the box clears the date; nonsense leaves the last good date. Optional dates (event start/end, copy's new start) offer Clear in the calendar.
- **S9-04 the Plan debug view keeps its native "Plan as of" date box**: it is behind the developer flag, not parent-facing.
- **S9-04 changed test expectations.** Date text is US style: `home.test` ("Wednesday, Oct 28", "Dec 8", "Dec 1", `longDate` "Saturday, Oct 31", `weekSpan` "Oct 26 – Nov 1"), `this-week`, `plan-debug` ("Nov 2 – Nov 8", "Oct 19 – Oct 25"), `track` ("Oct 19 – Oct 25", "Keep Dec 1", "Move to Dec 7"). Date boxes now hold "Nov 2, 2026", not "2026-11-02": `assignment-copy`, `assignments-bulk-add`, `timeline-edit-bar` read the formatted text; `timeline-drag` and `timeline-polish` read the box through `parseDateInput` to compare with the ISO dates. `timeline-edit-bar` and `timeline-add` list buttons: each date box now has a "Choose a date" calendar button (icon only), so the edit bar lists Close, Choose a date, Choose a date, Copy, More, and the Add form's buttons gain two unnamed icon buttons. `s8b-fixes` N11: the date padding rule it checked is replaced by the 44 px and min-width rule of `date-field.css`.
- **S9-05 one line, the existing words.** Home shows the single most important line across children (late or stalled work, then plan problems, then a heavy week; ties go to the earlier child) in the briefing's own wording ("Math 3 will finish about 3 weeks late."), and a "Review" action to Schedule · Week, where that line has its Move end / Keep buttons. Nothing renders when there is nothing, and a "Kept" line stays hidden on Home too. No check-in item (README). Home's old `showNudge` option is gone since Home no longer shows the Week briefing.
- **S9-05 changed test expectation.** `home.test`: the "no briefing" test looks for the new "Needs you" region instead of the Weekly briefing region (Home no longer renders the latter). `css-contrast.test`: `.home-briefing-dot` joins the non-text backgrounds (an 8 px dot behind no text). New `home-briefing.test.tsx`.
