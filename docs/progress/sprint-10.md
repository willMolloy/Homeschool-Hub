## Decisions

- **S10-01 one more layer than the ticket names.** The Year edit sheet is not modal, so it gets its own `--z-edit` (20): above the tab bar (10), below the undo bar (`--z-toast`, 30) so Undo stays tappable over it, and below modal sheets (50) and dialogs (60). Dialogs sit one step above sheets so a confirm opened from a sheet is on top. Local layers inside a card (the lane popover, the date calendar, the More menu) use `--z-raised` / `--z-popover`. Every `z-index` in the app's CSS now uses a token, and a test fails on a bare number. The edit sheet keeps its place above the tab bar for now (S10-06 restyles it).
- **S10-02 the All subjects strip is removed.** The mockup has none; each child's load ribbon in their header row does its job. The ribbons share one scale (the busiest child's heaviest point in the view is the tallest, 12 px phone / 14 px desktop).
- **S10-02 Together header has no ribbon.** Shared work already shows in every child's ribbon, so the Together row is just the icon and "Together".
- **S10-02 a Together bar's thickness** is the larger of the children's plans on each day (they only differ where one child has a day off), titled "Title · F M" (four initials, then "+N").
- **S10-02 every bar uses the subject's base colour step.** The per-child light/dark steps are dropped, since the group header already says whose lane it is; this matches the mockup.
- **S10-02 bar heights are capped at the mockup's maximum** (28 px phone, 32 px desktop; sub-rows at 0.8) until S10-04 brings in effort-based heights. Rows sit at a fixed pitch, so an outside name now goes only where it fits above its bar inside its own row, or is dropped (S10-04b reworks names).
- **S10-02 the sticky header** has a 90% page-colour background, so the month lines show faintly through it. Only the header takes taps, so you can't tap a bar through a stuck header.
- **S10-09 rail labels:** an icon rail with small labels under the icons. Decided by DeeDee on 2026-11-03.
- **S10-15 overlapping zones.** Rows are 36/40 px apart, so neighbouring 44 px zones overlap by 4–8 px. Each zone keeps its full 44 × 44 box (so the tap-target check measures 44), and the click handler sends a tap in the overlap to the nearer badge centre. A stuck group header sits above the zones, so a tap on a header never selects a badge.
- **S10-03 the stuck group header is now opaque** (S10-02 used 90%). Scrolled in the browser, bars passing under a stuck header showed through it and looked like ghost bars. The mockup's header is opaque too.
- **S10-03 the axis is opaque, and so is its foot rule.** Nothing scrolls visibly under it, as in the mockup.
- **S10-04 reviewed 2026-11-04, percentile change approved by DeeDee:** the view maximum is the 95th percentile of loads in the current view, not the single heaviest; loads above it draw at the maximum.
- **S10-04 the percentile is taken over each assignment's daily load** (not a lane's sum), because overlapping work sits in separate rows, so a bar's height is its own load. It covers every lane in the view across the whole year (Together once), not just the visible month, so flipping ‹ › through This month doesn't rescale. Year uses the smoothed loads, so smoothing and scale agree.
- **S10-04 the smoothing window is picked by span,** so custom views get it too: more than 26 weeks smooths like Year (15 school days), anything shorter that is still drawn smooth like a term (5). Daily and weekly detail are not smoothed.
- **S10-04 extra rows use the same curve,** topping out at 80% of the maximum (`EXTRA_ROW_FACTOR`) with the same minimum.
- **S10-04 the smooth look keeps its 3 px dead band** (a held height changes only when the target moves 3 px or more) so a lumpy day-to-day plan has no tiny jogs; the curve eases between held heights, so there are no steps. The even-pixel rounding and the slope limit are dropped (rounding could push a bar past `BAR_H_MAX`; the moving average already keeps changes gradual). `calmSeries`/`limitSlope` removed from `smooth.ts`.
- **S10-04 heights are relative,** so a uniformly light plan looks the same as a uniformly heavy one; the difference shows between assignments and weeks in the same view. A child shown alone is rescaled to their own loads (expected; noted for the S10-10a tester).
- **S10-04 the ribbons use the same curve,** with their maximum at the 95th percentile of the children's daily totals.
- **S10-04 the dev override:** `bars=min,max,curve` (any part may be blank; bad parts are ignored). Raising the max grows the lane pitch (max + 8) and the sub-row step so bars never touch. Parsed only when `useDebugFlag()` is true (a dev build with `?debug=1`).
- **S10-04 outside names are rarer now:** with an 8 px minimum and a fixed pitch, few bars leave room above them, so most names sit inside or are dropped until S10-04b reworks names. The T-11d tests use a deliberately light lane.
- **S10-04b outside names go right, never above:** the mockup's rule (full name to the right of the bar in muted text, when the row is free) replaces T-11d's names above thin bars, so rows no longer grow a label lane. An outside name is never cut; if the full name doesn't fit beside the bar, it falls back to a cut name inside.
- **S10-04b an inside name still needs a bar at least 13 px thick** (`NAME_PATCH_PX`, T-11c) so the text always has the bar behind it; thinner bars name outside or not at all.
- **S10-04b "at least 6 letters" counts characters before the "…"** (spaces included, a trailing space trimmed), matching the mockup's `fit()`. DeeDee's earlier T-11 "down to one character" rule is replaced by the ticket.
- **S10-04b free room** for an outside name ends at the next piece of any bar in the same row (including the same bar's piece after a break) or at the plot's right edge, less 4 px. Names of different rows never meet because rows don't overlap.
- **S10-04b outside names no longer dim when another bar is selected:** muted text at the 0.7 dim opacity would fall below AA. Inside names already stayed at full strength (T-11d).
- **S10-04b sub-row labels use the same 12 px size** (the mockup draws them 1 px smaller); one size keeps the measure and the fit rules simple. Logged for the S10-10a tester.
- **S10-04b Frank's Fall view:** the 47 px piece of Black Beauty before Thanksgiving now has no name (six letters and "…" don't fit, and the break is too narrow for the name outside); the piece after the break shows "Black Beauty" in full.
- **S10-04c the zones were already 44 × 44** (T-03's `tapBox`); the gap was the first lane under each group header, whose zone reached up to 4 px under the header (which blocks taps). That box now starts at the header's foot. Zones still overlap neighbouring rows (rows are 28–40 px apart); there the nearer centreline wins, as the ticket asks, so the area that picks a given bar is the 44 px box less its neighbours' nearer halves.
- **S10-04c at the plot's left edge the badge still wins** (S10-15), and outside labels (S10-04b) are not tap targets of their own beyond the bar's zone; both noted for the S10-10a tester.
- **S10-05 the break colour is `--tl-muted`**, which is the mockup's `--brk` in both themes (#F2ECE1 / #302C27). The token is named `--tl-wash` to match the other timeline tokens.
- **S10-05 breaks are a band and a wash at every zoom**, not only Year: the hatch at Fall/Month is dropped so the bars read the same through a break everywhere. Each break's label tooltip moved to the wash, which is on top.
- **S10-05 which gaps are bridged:** only a gap with work on both sides whose school days are all inside a break for that bar (family-wide, or one naming the bar's child). A gap of weekends only, a gap with any open school day, and the ends of an assignment stay gaps, so connectors still show real pauses. The ribbons and the 95th-percentile max use the unbridged load, so a break still reads as no work in the child's ribbon.
- **S10-05 fix: idle days are per assignment and come from the inputs,** not from zeros in the plan, so a day with no work for any other reason (a light day rounded to nothing, unplaced work) still counts as open and keeps a real pause. Days off that name the child are still the "closed" side, not idle. A gap of only idle days and weekends is never bridged.
- **S10-06 pace rounding:** units are whole from 1 a day up and to one decimal below (never under 0.1); minutes are to the nearest 5 from 10 up and whole below (at least 1). Without a weight the line has no minutes ("About 3 pages a day").
- **S10-06 the pace is the plan's,** not a plain division by weekdays: the same `/api/plan/pace` count of real days the Add form uses, at the dates showing in the tiles, so it moves as a date changes. Minutes use the assignment's weight, else the subject default, else the built-in.
- **S10-06 the outline is the subject's strong colour at 2 px;** other bars still dim when one is chosen (T-11d kept).
- **S10-06 "native date fields" in the ticket was stale:** the sheet already used `DateField`; it is restyled as the mockup's tiles. Under 360 px the two tiles stack. The subtitle (subject · children) stays as it was; the mockup's units total is not added.
- **S10-07 only the Year view's Add sheet changes.** The Assignments page form keeps its dropdown, checkboxes and boxes (forms work is S10-16 in 10b, and many subject tests drive the dropdown).
- **S10-07 Unit stays a native select:** it is not one of the ticket's four controls.
- **S10-07 the picker button is named "Subject: <current>"** (or "Choose a subject…"); the common subjects are listed in the picker before "New subject…", as in the dropdown.
- **S10-07 the chip initial is drawn by CSS** (`data-initial`) so each chip's label, and its checkbox's name, is just the child's name. The on-state avatar uses the card colour so it shows on the soft chip.
- **S10-08 the "+" shows its words from 640 px** ("+ Add assignment" pill, the desktop mockup); below that it is the round icon. Its accessible name is "Add assignment" either way.
- **S10-08 the "+" is only on the Year view** (Week has no add there today). The button is rendered by the timeline into the title row through a portal rather than lifting the add state into SchedulePage (smallest change).
- **S10-08 the pencil's name is "Edit views"** (ticket) — the mockup's "Edit terms" was not used; the tap-target smoke script still finds it by `.tl-zoom-edit`.
