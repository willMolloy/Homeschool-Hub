## Decisions

- **S12-01 the day is validated against the start, not the end:** finished can have `endedOn` before the planned end (finished early) or after it (finished late); only "before the start" is refused.
- **S12-01 stopped with no day is today, or the start if that is later** (a parent stopping next year's book): never a day before the start.
- **S12-09 clearing the year leaves its breaks and term views,** and says so in the question: they were made by the parent's earlier choice and Days off is where they are edited (the same rule as changing a shape in S11-03).
- **S12-09 the reminder rule is kept on the server,** not in Home: `/api/me` carries no school year, and the API dropping `year` from the deferred list whenever a year is saved makes the reminder and the saved year agree for every client.
- **S12-09 N9 is fixed in words and width, not by moving the banner beside an icon:** the icons are in the picture's left column and the banner stays in the flow above it (S11b-03: a banner over the picture covered bars), so the text says "in the left column".
- **S12-07 the scenario answers the demo's poems through the list too,** instead of reseeding a family with exactly two questions, so it keeps working while the real date moves and more of the demo's books end (it answers "We stopped" for every other row it finds).
- **S12-06 Change offers the question's three choices plus Not ended,** as the ticket says. For an assignment that already ended, **Not yet** means "it isn't over": it opens it again and moves its end; **Not ended** opens it again without moving the end. Only an ended assignment shows the row (an open one is answered from Home when its end passes).
- **S12-05 the dot uses `--tl-primary`,** the fern accent every other accent in the app uses (the badge dot on the briefing, the primary button); `--tl-accent` is a near-background wash that would not show as a dot.
- **S12-05 the shell fetches its own copy of the assignments and children** rather than sharing Home's, because the nav sits outside the page and the dot must clear on answers made anywhere (including Undo); the cost is two small GETs per screen change.
- **S12-04 a finished bar keeps its whole planned length even when it was finished early:** the ticket says the bar keeps its place; what ends at `endedOn` is the *work* (Week bars, check-in weeks and heavy-week counts ignore days after it), not the picture.
- **S12-04 stopped keeps the pace it had** (days before `endedOn` are as planned) and drops the rest, rather than squeezing all the units into the shorter span.
- **S12-04 ended bars use the existing lighter colour step** instead of a new opacity, so the in-bar name keeps its AA label colour; stopped is also desaturated and dashed so the two read apart without colour alone (the words "Stopped" and the check carry the meaning).
- **S12-03 every answer goes through the sheet's date step,** so Yes, We stopped and Not yet each show the day they will save and can change it before Save (the ticket asks for the editable day on Yes and We stopped, and a picker on Not yet); it is one more tap than answering with the default, and Undo and S12-06's Change cover the rest.
- **S12-03 Not yet suggests a week from today** (the ticket says only "pick a new end"); the field says the question comes back after the day, and a day before today is refused because it would ask again at once.
- **S12-03 a finished or stopped day can't be after today,** and can't be before the start (the API refuses the latter too).
- **S12-03 the card's accent is the subject's strong colour, the fern for several at once,** and the card is a clear bordered card with a 6 px rule, not the briefing's soft pill, so it reads as a question and not a line of news.
- **S12-02 the demo's older books are marked finished, not a new assignment added:** the demo's Aug–Oct books all ended before Nov 4, so left open they would all be questions; answering the old ones keeps "exactly one waiting" true, with the poems (Margaret) as the one that ended last week.
- **S12-02 a family with an assignment whose children are all archived gets no question,** so the dot never lights for something the parent cannot answer sensibly.
- **S12-01 the engine fields are added now** (as inputs, unused) so the plan input is read in this ticket, as its step 2 says; the plan changes in S12-04.
