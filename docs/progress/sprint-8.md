## Decisions (conservative calls on ambiguous points)

- **S8-01 one palette, eight slots.** The old "safe" (Okabe-Ito, 10 subjects, three steps) and "vivid" palettes are replaced by the eight Soft Paper hues (honey, sky, mint, butter, blue, coral, lilac, sage). `PALETTE_SLOTS` is 8, so the timeline deals slots 1-8 then repeats (a 9th subject reuses honey). Nothing in the app switched to "vivid" (no UI), so `applyTimelinePalette`, `data-palette` and their test are deleted.
- **S8-01 the colour-blind distance tests are gone.** The pastel fills are deliberately close in lightness, so the T-01b pairwise-distance floor (and the T-11c nudge tables) no longer apply. Replaced by: ink on every fill, the bar's amount text, strong tone against fill (3:1), each child step's label (AA). Subjects are told apart by their icon (S8-11), never by colour alone.
- **S8-01 contrast numbers.** Light fills: ink at least 9:1 and the bar's amount (ink at 78%) at least 5.2:1, as the README says. The mockup's own dark fills give ink 6.8-7.4:1 on honey and butter, so dark asserts at least 6.5:1 and the amount text is ink at 86% in dark (`--tl-bar-secondary-opacity` per theme) to hold 5.2:1. Dark field borders (`input`) were lightened to `#7A7266` to reach 3:1.
- **S8-01 bars are solid.** `--tl-bar-opacity` is 1 (T-08c's 0.91 is gone: the fills already carry the 14%/10% mix). Dimmed bars (selection) go from 0.65 to 0.7 so page text on them keeps AA with the new ink.
- **S8-01 swatch mapping** (the stored colour is unchanged; it is only drawn differently by `ColorDot` and the picker chips): Ochre to honey, Teal to sky, Green to mint, Pink to butter, Blue to blue, Brick to coral, Purple to lilac, Slate to sage. Timeline lanes keep dealing slots by display order (as before), not by the subject's stored colour, so existing timeline behaviour and tests are unchanged.
- **S8-01 child steps.** A subject drawn once per child uses the fill (base), the fill 40% toward the card (light, 14% in dark) and the fill 16% toward its strong tone (dark, 10% in dark).
- **S8-01 changed test expectations.** `palette.test.ts` rewritten for the new generator (reasons above); `css-contrast.test.ts` reads colours through the generated tokens (the app CSS now uses `var(--tl-*)`), the desktop nav's translucent active fill went from 0.16 to 0.1 white to keep AA on fern; `timeline-opacity.test.ts` (solid bars, dim 0.7, all swatches AA when dimmed); `timeline-model.test.ts` (slots repeat after 8, not 10).
- **S8-02 System sets no attribute.** Light and Dark set `data-theme` on `<html>`; System removes it, so the tokens follow `prefers-color-scheme` (`:root:not([data-theme="light"])`). The pre-paint script in `index.html` only needs to act for a saved Light or Dark. It also paints the page colour (cream, or charcoal under the device's dark setting) before the stylesheet loads.
- **S8-02 where Appearance lives.** On Family settings (a card under the form, applies at once, no Save). Home's avatar menu (S8-04) links to Family settings; it does not duplicate the control.
- **S8-02 changed test expectation.** `css-contrast.test.ts` "declares the app light-only" is now "lets native controls follow the theme": `color-scheme: light dark` on `:root`, `light` / `dark` under the two `data-theme` values, and `light dark` in the meta tag (the whole point of the ticket).
- **S8-02 dark contrast of older screen CSS** is checked in S8-03, which replaces their hard-coded colours with tokens.
- **S8-03 one set of classes, components on top.** The older screens already share the classes `.btn`, `.btn-secondary`, `.card`, `.field`, `.choice`, so restyling those classes (primary fern pill, secondary cream pill, 14 px hairline card, 12 px fields) is the like-for-like swap for the 90-odd existing buttons; I did not rewrite each `<button className="btn">` into `<Button>`. The new components (`Button`, `Card`, `Chip`, `Segmented`, `ListRow`, `Field`, `SelectField`) wrap the same classes and are what the new screens (S8-04 on) use. Added `.btn-quiet` for quiet text buttons.
- **S8-03 dates stay native.** The ticket allows a calm calendar popover "where the native one is ugly". The native date input now takes the theme through `color-scheme` and the Soft Paper field style; no custom calendar was built (smallest choice, keeps every validation and test as is).
- **S8-03 hex to tokens.** Every hard-coded white, grey and blue in the screen CSS is now a token (`--tl-card`, `--tl-secondary`, `--tl-input`, `--tl-primary`...). Two new token pairs cover the two colours the old CSS had no token for: `destructive-soft` (error panels) and `notice` / `notice-foreground` (the engine-mismatch banner).
- **S8-03 test change.** `css-contrast.test.ts` now runs its checks in both themes (light, then dark with the dark tokens applied), so every solid background in the app CSS is checked for AA in dark as well; the "catches the wrong colours" self-test picks the opposite pair per theme.
- **S8-04 routes.** `/` Home, `/track`, `/schedule` (`?view=week|year`, default from `hh.schedule.view`, else Week), `/setup` and `/setup/{children,subjects,assignments,days-off,events,settings}`. Old links redirect and keep their query: `/timeline` to `/schedule?view=year`, `/kids`, `/subjects`, `/schedule/assignments|days-off|events|settings`. The redirects are a table in `layout/nav.tsx`.
- **S8-04 Week | Year are tabs, not toggle buttons** (`role="tablist"`, `aria-selected`), so a "Year" tab and the timeline's own "Year" zoom button never collide for assistive tech or tests.
- **S8-04 Home's greeting** is "Good morning / afternoon / evening" from the device clock, without a name: the app stores a family name ("Demo family"), not a parent's, and "Good morning, Demo family" reads wrong. The family name is the title of the family button's sheet and its initial is the button.
- **S8-04 "Week N of M"** comes from the span of the live assignments (first start to last end; weeks counted from the first start), shown only while today is inside it. The app stores no school-year dates (S7b-05), so this is the closest honest figure.
- **S8-04 Track work card.** Per child, the bar is the share of this week's planned work that lies before today (the plan assumes it was done) and "N left" counts assignments with planned work today or later this week ("All done" at 0). No effort numbers. The "Last week needs a quick check" line is the card's own, so the briefing's old nudge line is hidden on Home (`showNudge={false}`); elsewhere the nudge now links to `/track`.
- **S8-04 Schedule card.** "On track to finish {date}" when the latest effective end of open work equals its planned end, otherwise "finishing about {date}"; the thumbnail is one soft bar per subject across the span of the assignments, with today.
- **S8-04 Appearance and Sign out.** The family button opens a sheet (a bottom sheet on phones) with a Family settings row and Sign out; Appearance stays on Family settings (S8-02 decision), so it is one tap further.
- **S8-04 interim content.** Until S8-05 and S8-06, Schedule's Week shows the old This Week content (switcher, briefing, last-week card, Make an adjustment) and Track work shows the old last-week card. Both are replaced by their own tickets.
- **S8-04 page names.** The Kids page is now titled "Children" (the Set up row says Children). The "‹ Schedule" back link on every set-up screen is now "‹ Set up".
- **S8-04 changed test expectations.** `app-shell.test.tsx` (three tabs, active state, one tap home, no header or Sign out outside Home); `router.test.tsx` (new routes and every old link's landing); `auth.test.tsx` (the family name is no longer in a header: the family button; Sign out is in its sheet; /kids lands on /setup/children); `settings-form.test.tsx` (the new name reaches Home's family button, not a header); the tests of the old Plan page (`this-week`, `plan-debug`, `week-adjustment`, `last-week-card`, `briefing`) now open `/schedule?view=week` and find the heading "Schedule" instead of "Plan" (same assertions); `briefing.test.tsx` (the nudge links to `/track`); the timeline tests (`timeline-view`, `timeline-zoom`, `timeline-child-filter`) expect the page title "Schedule" and `?view=year` in the URL; `undo-bar.test.tsx` (leaves the page by the Track work tab); `kids-list.test.tsx` and the heading "Kids" in the kids tests become "Children". `schedule-hub.test.tsx` is replaced by `home.test.tsx` (Set up list and its back link). `scripts/smoke/scenarios/tap-targets.mjs` now lists the new routes (not run in this commit; run once at the end).
- **S8-05 where the bars come from.** A current or coming week's bars are built from `/api/plan` for that week with the engine's `plannedRangeForWeek` (units before the week, units at its end), so the range is the plan's own: "pages 120-150", "lessons 31-35", "chapter 9", "150 minutes" (fractions round outward, never "4.38"). Bars are ordered by child, then subject order, then title; only assignments with work that week get one.
- **S8-05 no check on Schedule's Week.** The ticket says each bar has a round check "S8-06 wires it", but checking in mid-week is out of scope (S8-06) and the Soft Paper mockup's Schedule bars have no check. The check lives on the bars of Track work (finished weeks); the current and coming weeks have none.
- **S8-05 a bar opens the child's adjustment.** Tapping any bar opens the existing S6-03 sheet (`WeekAdjustment`) for that bar's child, which lists all of that child's assignments for the week (the sheet and its batch save are unchanged). The old per-student "Make an adjustment" buttons are gone.
- **S8-05 past weeks.** Until S8-06, a finished week on Schedule shows a line pointing to Track work. S8-06 makes it the same check-in bars as Track work.
- **S8-05 child choice** is kept in `localStorage` (`hh.child`), shared by the Week view and Track work; "Both" is only offered with several children; a saved child that is gone or archived falls back to the first one. A single child gets no switch.
- **S8-05 events** appear as "{days} · {name}" (for example "Tue · Co-op"), one quiet line each, for events that overlap the week and apply to that child or the whole family. The mockup's "(Literature skipped)" detail is not shown: it would need each event's rule per subject spelled out, which is more than one quiet line.
- **S8-05 debug.** The plan table (`PlanDebugView`) shows under the bars only with the developer flag (`?debug=1` in a dev build), as before; parents never see it.
- **S8-05 changed test expectations.** `this-week.test.tsx` is rewritten for the new view (the T-03b header and the per-student check-in sheet it covered are gone; the shownWeek and childWeek unit tests are kept). `plan-debug.test.tsx`: "This week" is now the page's level-2 heading and "Coming week" / "Last week" follow it, the dates read "2 Nov - 8 Nov"; a failed plan load shows "Couldn't load this week." with Retry; an active child with nothing planned shows "Nothing planned this week." in their section (found with the Both choice), and archived children still do not show. `week-adjustment.test.tsx` opens the sheet by tapping a bar, and its "a past week still uses the check-in sheet" test is removed (S8-06 covers past weeks with Update status). `briefing.test.tsx`: the page-loaded signal is the child's heading, and the nudge test now follows the link to Track work. `last-week-card.test.tsx` opens `/track` (where the old card lives until S8-06).
