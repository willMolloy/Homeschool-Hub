## Decisions

- **S11-01 the redirect only applies to `/`** (Home), as the ticket says ("signing in, or opening `/`"). A deep link such as `/schedule` opens as it always did; the family is not trapped in the flow.
- **S11-01 "Finish later" lasts for the sign-in, not the page.** The ticket says the flow "comes back at next sign-in until done", so Home has to be reachable in the meantime; the flag lives in the tab (sessionStorage) and is cleared on sign-in and sign-out.
- **S11-01 `setupDone: false` is the only thing that sends a family into the flow** (`=== false`, not "falsy"), so an older API answer without the field never traps anyone.
- **S11-01 the "all set" screen is shown before the final save lands,** because once `/api/me` says done, nothing else would keep `/welcome` from sending the family Home.
- **S11-01 the step is saved on Back too,** so closing the tab right after going back resumes where the parent was, not where they had been.
- **S11-02 the family name starts as it is,** not blank: it is the visible default (the name the family was created with), so a parent who is happy with it just taps Continue.
- **S11-02 children are saved as they are added,** not all together on Continue, so a parent who closes the tab keeps them and comes back to a list. There is no remove button in the flow; a typo is fixed in Set up > Children afterwards.
- **S11-02 the school-day chips' legend is visually hidden** (still read by screen readers): the question above them is already their label, and a drawn box and legend repeated it.
- **S11-03 the year's two days are stored on the family** (`school_year_start` / `_end`), which the ticket does not list: without them the flow cannot resume at the breaks step (which needs the year's range) after the tab is closed, and Back could not show what was typed.
- **S11-03 no holidays here.** The shapes add only their own breaks (the pattern's break weeks); US holidays are the breaks step's (S11-04, none ticked by default). So "36 weeks", which has no pattern, adds nothing here, and its preview says so.
- **S11-03 Fall and Spring writes the two views** (Fall to Dec 31, Spring from Jan 1, via the S9-06 overrides) rather than saving nothing, so the zoom row is right before any work exists to draw a grid from. A year that never crosses New Year is left unsplit.
- **S11-03 Three terms are the stretches between the pattern's first two breaks,** so a term view never includes its break; a year too short for two breaks is cut into thirds. Fall and Spring are hidden (the row would otherwise show five overlapping views).
- **S11-03 applying a shape again (Back, then Continue) leaves earlier breaks where they are** (it only adds what is not already a day off, and replaces earlier "Term n" views); changing from Three terms to 36 weeks does not remove the first shape's breaks. Days off is where they are edited.
- **S11-03 the reminder opens the step on its own** (`/welcome?only=year`) rather than the Days off screen, because nothing in Set up lets a parent give the year's days; a deferred year is finished where it was put off.
