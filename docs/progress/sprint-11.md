## Decisions

- **S11-01 the redirect only applies to `/`** (Home), as the ticket says ("signing in, or opening `/`"). A deep link such as `/schedule` opens as it always did; the family is not trapped in the flow.
- **S11-01 "Finish later" lasts for the sign-in, not the page.** The ticket says the flow "comes back at next sign-in until done", so Home has to be reachable in the meantime; the flag lives in the tab (sessionStorage) and is cleared on sign-in and sign-out.
- **S11-01 `setupDone: false` is the only thing that sends a family into the flow** (`=== false`, not "falsy"), so an older API answer without the field never traps anyone.
- **S11-01 the "all set" screen is shown before the final save lands,** because once `/api/me` says done, nothing else would keep `/welcome` from sending the family Home.
- **S11-01 the step is saved on Back too,** so closing the tab right after going back resumes where the parent was, not where they had been.
