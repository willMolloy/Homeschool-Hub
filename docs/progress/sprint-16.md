## Decisions

- **S16-01 one rule for screen and paper:** Days reads `buildSheet`, it does not recompute ranges. The comparison test renders both the Days view and `/print/week` for the same week and compares (child, day, title, range) sets.
- **S16-01 the load bar** is the day's total effort (`DayLoad.thickness`, summed over the children shown) against the week's busiest day, so it shows the shape of the week, not an amount; it is `aria-hidden` and has no numbers. (A flat week shows every bar full.)
- **S16-01 the content column is widened from 840 px for Days** (`.shell-main:has(.days-grid)`, like Year): the 640 px cap would leave five columns of about 120 px.
- **S16-01 events ("Tue · Co-op") are not on Days:** the ticket lists none; they stay on the Bars view.
- **S16-01 a day that is off for one child under All children** adds a quiet "Frank · Dentist" line beside the other child's work; if every shown child is off, the day is one line (names joined once).
- **S16-01 a finished (past) week** can also be shown as Days (same plans as the print sheet); a line opens Update status as its bar does.
- **S16-01 `ChildPlan` days after `endedOn`** are dropped through `buildSheet` (the sheet already does) and from the load sum.

- **S16-02 the toggle is its own row,** not beside the week arrows: Week | Year, the arrows and a third control do not fit at 360 px, and the ticket allows wrapping.
- **S16-02 `?layout=` beats the saved choice** (like `?view=`), so a link can open either; picking a layout sets both.
- **S16-05 no API change:** assignments and entries are already on the Records page (and allowed for viewers), so Books finished and per-child school days are computed in the web with one helper that Records, Term in review and the CSV share. The API's `books` (typed titles) and `schoolDays` are unchanged; the single-child card still shows the API's number, which equals the helper's.
- **S16-05 Books finished means reading units only:** pages and chapters (the ticket's "unit pages or chapters"); a finished lessons or minutes assignment is not a book.
- **S16-05 Term in review's card now has both lists:** FINISHED (every finished assignment, as in Sprint 13) and BOOKS FINISHED (its reading ones), so a finished book can appear in both. Left as is (layout and its tests untouched); Will may want the two merged.
- **S16-05 the Books finished CSV column is in the Totals file only** (a child's range), not Each entry.
- **S16-06 "reading subjects first"** is read as reading assignments (pages or chapters), the same rule as Books finished, because subjects have no type.
- **S16-06 the recent row** uses the children picked in the form (none picked: no row); in an edit it is that entry's child.
- **S16-07 Together** is an assignment of two or more of the children shown, and it is merged into one line only when every child's row for that day is identical (same range); if the ranges differ the lines stay under each child.
- **S16-07 the child's colour** is a rule under the heading, not the text colour, so the heading text keeps its contrast whatever colour the parent picked.
- **S16-08 capital letter:** the span words use the ticket's capital ("Chapter 17 · start"); whole-unit ranges stay lower-case as in S15-06.
- **S16-08 "day 2 of 3" is inferred** from a day's size because a daily sheet or a week's plan does not carry the days around it; it is exact at a steady pace.
- **S16-09 N4:** the card keeps the Finished list (every finished assignment) and the books count as a number, as the ticket's default says; Will may still choose the other way round.
- **S16-10 total hours** is a big number of its own only when the log tracks total hours; with hours by subject alone the total is on the bars ("in all"), so a "Hours by subject" card is just the bars.
- **S16-10 the Core / non-core template also tracks hours by subject,** so its card is the two numbers plus the bars, as the log does.
- **S16-10 supersedes S16-09 N4** (the single FINISHED list): the card no longer has any finished list.
