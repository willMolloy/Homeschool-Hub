## Decisions

- **S15-01 data model:** a membership table (`family_member`) beside the existing `parent` table, not a new `viewer` table. A `parent` row is now "a person with a login" (the table keeps its Sprint 14 name; a rename would touch every test and the CLI for no gain), and the role is on the membership. One person can be a parent of their own family and a viewer of any number of others, and a co-op is just another family.
- **S15-01 the insert trigger:** a database trigger gives every new `parent` row with a family a parent membership, so no code path can create a parent without one (and about thirty older fixtures did not need to change). A viewer's membership is made by the API.
- **S15-01 removing a viewer who has nowhere else to go deletes their login** (no membership, no home family): nothing about them is kept, and the same email can sign up again from a new link. A viewer who also has their own family just loses that one membership.
- **S15-01 a removed or departed viewer is signed out of that account only.** Their session for it is deleted (the foreign key), so the next request is 401; on a device with another account they sign in again and land there. Leaving is gentler: the device is moved to their next account.
- **S15-01 viewer links need a family, and "existing" joins are for viewer links only.** A person who already has a login can't be made a second parent of a family by a link (Sprint 14 had no such thing), so a parent link redeemed that way gets the plain unavailable message and is not used up.
- **S15-01 already a member:** 409 `already_member` ("You already have access to this account.") and the link is kept; they proved who they are, so there is nothing to hide.
- **S15-01 `session` is checked in `preValidation`, not `preHandler`,** so a missing session (401) and a viewer's write (403) are decided before a body is looked at: a viewer sending garbage learns nothing more than one sending a valid body.
- **S15-01 the two account calls (`switch`, `leave`) are the only non-GETs a viewer may make;** both act on the viewer's own membership and change no family data. Everything else stays 403. The ticket says "every non-GET returns 403"; these are the one deliberate exception (the ticket itself asks for switching and leaving).
- **S15-01 the first link guess is as costly as a sign-in:** the join calls keep Sprint 14's throttle (30 looks and 10 sign-ups a minute per address).
